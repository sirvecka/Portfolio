02 / BRAND STORYTELLING

More than
a restaurant.

What makes a family-owned restaurant special isn't just what's on the menu, but the people and stories behind it.

At Michaelangelo's, family is the foundation of the business. Led by two generations of mothers, Liz and Nicollette, the restaurant represents years of dedication and hard work.

For Mother's Day, I developed a storytelling concept that put the family at the center rather than creating a traditional promotional reel.

I layered heartfelt voice messages from their children over footage of a surprise celebration featuring chocolates, cupcakes, and gifts at the restaurant.

The goal was to humanize the brand, celebrate the women behind the business, and create an emotional connection beyond the dining experience.

03 / INFLUENCER MARKETING

Introducing a
new strategy.

Before my involvement, Michaelangelo's had never implemented influencer collaborations as part of its marketing strategy.

I identified an opportunity to introduce the restaurant to new audiences through NYC-based food and lifestyle creators.

I helped establish the restaurant's first creator partnerships by researching influencers, initiating outreach, discussing collaboration opportunities, coordinating content, and reviewing deliverables for brand alignment.
